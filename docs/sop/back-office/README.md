# Back office procedures

For the back office: the order desk, accounts and HR people who work in the web portal rather than the field. The Back office role sees the whole organisation; it processes orders and fulfilment, confirms collections, keeps balances and statements, records stock, runs imports and exports, reads reports, pays expense claims and keeps payroll inputs. It doesn't decide approvals or work in the field. Some steps here need a permission the Back office role doesn't hold by default, such as running the statutory pay run; each procedure says which, and your admin may have changed the roles.

| Procedure | What it is for | Who |
|---|---|---|
| [Process orders](process-orders.md) | Read the register, take an order at the desk, record fulfilment, cancel; where an order is decided | Back office, Manager, Business head, Admin |
| [Confirm collections](confirm-collections.md) | Confirm or reject what the field collected, mark bounced cheques, match receipts, print a receipt | Back office, Admin |
| [Keep receivables and statements](receivables-and-statements.md) | Balances, ageing, statement entries, invoices and receipts, and an account's Finance section | Back office, Business head, Admin |
| [Keep distributors' stock](stock.md) | Record goods in and out, count stock, read the stock reports | Back office, Business head, Admin |
| [Import and export](imports-and-exports.md) | Load records from a spreadsheet; download exports | Back office, Admin |
| [Reports](reports.md) | Read and download the standard reports, every report in one workbook, scheduled reports | Back office, Manager, Business head, Admin, Viewer |
| [Pay expense claims](expense-payouts.md) | Read the month's claims, mark approved claims paid, export for payroll | Back office, Business head, Admin |
| [Payroll](payroll.md) | Monthly gross and the attendance-based estimate; the statutory pay run | Back office, Admin |
