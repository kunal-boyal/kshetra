# Follow today's plan

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Field user, Manager (`visit.use`) |
| **Where** | Field app › **Home** › **Today's plan**; Field app › **Plan** |
| **Module** | Core |
| **Related** | [Visit an account](visit-an-account.md), [Start your day](start-your-day.md) |

Your plan is the accounts you are meant to visit each day, in route order, as your manager planned them: planned visits (Beats in the agri and FMCG templates) in your journey plan (Beat plan in agri, Tour plan in pharma, PJP in FMCG). You can also add a visit to today yourself.

## Before you start

- Your manager has planned your month, or given you a route. Where your organisation approves journey plans, only an approved month reaches the phone.
- The plan is kept on the phone, so it reads offline. Changes your manager makes arrive with the next sync.

## Steps

### Today's plan on Home

1. Open **Home**. Under the greeting is today's date and, when today's plan follows a route, the route's name.
2. The **Today's plan** card says how many stops are done (**… of … done**), with a bar: done, and visited but waiting for review.
3. The next stop shows with its number, **Next**, the account's type and, once you are on duty with a fix, how far away it is. Its purpose, if your manager gave one, shows beside it. Tap it to open the account.
4. When every stop is done, the card says **Every stop for today is done.** With nothing planned, it says **Nothing planned for today.**
5. Tap **See the whole plan** to open the **Plan** tab.

### The Plan tab

1. Open **Plan**. The heading is your organisation's word for the journey plan. Where months are approved, an approved day shows **Approved**.
2. Under **Day**, choose yesterday, **Today** or tomorrow.
3. The map shows the day's stops, numbered, with the route between them and, today, where you are (**You are here**). The summary says how many stops and how many kilometres the route runs.
4. Below the map, each stop is a row with its number, account, type and purpose, and where it stands: **Done**, **In review**, **Missed**, **Next** or **Planned**. A stop without a known place says **no place known** and isn't on the map.
5. Tap a stop to open its account and check in there ([Visit an account](visit-an-account.md)).

The list says everything the map shows, so the tab works without a map.

### Add a visit to today

1. On **Plan**, tap **Add an unplanned visit**, or on **Home** under **Start something**, tap **Unplanned visit**. Either opens your accounts.
2. Open the account.
3. On its **Visit** card, tap **Add to today's plan**. The card says **Added to today's plan.**
4. Until it uploads, the stop shows on **Plan** and on Home as **Planned on this phone, waiting to send**.

You don't have to plan a visit to make it: you can simply check in at any account you look after. Adding it to the plan makes it count in today's plan.

## What the system checks

- A planned visit becomes visited when you check in there with it, and done once what you submitted in that visit is approved; a rejection puts it back to planned, and a stop still planned by the end of the day becomes missed (BR-35).
- Where journey plans need approval, only approved days reach the phone (BR-35).
- You can add a visit only for an account you look after. The app doesn't offer **Add to today's plan** for an account already planned today.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Nothing planned for today.** | No visits are planned for you today, or the month isn't approved yet | Ask your manager, or add an unplanned visit |
| **This could not be saved on the phone. Try again.** | Adding to today's plan failed on the phone | Try again |
