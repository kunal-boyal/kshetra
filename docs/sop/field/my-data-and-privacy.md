# Your data and privacy

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Everyone using the field app |
| **Where** | Field app › **More** › **Privacy**; Field app › **More** › **My data**; the sign-in screen › **Privacy** |
| **Module** | Core |
| **Related** | [Install the app and sign in](install-and-sign-in.md), [Start your day](start-your-day.md) |

The privacy notice says what the app records about you, what it never records, who sees it and how long it is kept. Under **My data** you can ask your organisation for a copy of your personal data, a correction, or its erasure once you have left.

## Before you start

- The privacy notice reads offline, signed in or not.
- Data requests need signal.

## Steps

### Read the privacy notice

1. Open **More** › **Privacy**, or tap **Privacy** on the sign-in screen.
2. **Privacy notice** says when it last changed (**Last changed on ….**) and has these sections: **Who is responsible**, **What is recorded**, **What is never recorded**, **What it is used for**, **Who sees it**, **Where and for how long**, **Your rights** and **Security**.
3. Signed in, where your organisation has its own policy, **Your organisation’s privacy policy** opens it.

### Ask about your data

1. Open **More** › **My data**.
2. Read **Ask your organisation for a copy of your personal data, to correct it, or to erase it once you have left. They answer within 30 days.**
3. Under **Ask**, choose **A copy of my data**, **A correction to my data** or **Erasing my data**.
4. Under **Anything to add**, write what you want, such as which detail is wrong (up to 1,000 characters).
5. Tap **Send request**. It says **Sent. Your organisation answers by ….**
6. **Your requests** lists each with its kind and state: **Open** with **Answer due by …**, or **Done** or **Refused** with **Their answer: …**.

Your organisation's admins answer the request on the portal. A copy is prepared as a file for them to pass on; erasure removes your personal details and keeps the business records, and only once you have left.

## What the system checks

- You may have one open request of each kind at a time.
- The answer is due 30 days after the request; admins are reminded before it is due.
- An erasure can be completed only once your user has been deactivated.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Connect to the internet to see or send requests.** | No signal | Move to signal and **Try again** |
| **Some details need fixing. Check the highlighted fields.** | You already have an open request of that kind | Wait for its answer, or choose another kind |
| **The server could not be reached. Check your connection, then try again.** | No connection when sending | Try again with signal |
