# Your data and privacy

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Everyone using the field app |
| **Where** | Field app › **More** › **Privacy**; Field app › **More** › **My data**; the sign-in screen › **Privacy** |
| **Module** | Core |
| **Related** | [Install the app and sign in](install-and-sign-in.md), [Start your day](start-your-day.md) |

The privacy notice says what the app records about you, what it never records, who sees it and how long it is kept. Under **My data** you can ask your organisation for a copy of your personal data, a correction, or its erasure once you have left, and download your copy once they have answered.

## Before you start

- The privacy notice reads offline, signed in or not.
- Data requests, and downloading your copy, need signal.

## Steps

### Read the privacy notice

1. Open **More** › **Privacy**, or tap **Privacy** on the sign-in screen.
2. **Privacy notice** says when it last changed (**Last changed on ….**) and has these sections: **Who is responsible**, **What is recorded**, **What is never recorded**, **What it is used for**, **Who sees it**, **Where and for how long**, **Your rights** and **Security**.
3. Signed in, where your organisation has its own policy, **Your organisation’s privacy policy** opens it.

### Ask about your data

1. Open **More** › **My data**.
2. Read **Ask your organisation for a copy of your personal data, to correct it, or to erase it once you have left. They answer within 30 days.**
3. Under **Ask**, choose **A copy of my data**, **A correction to my data** or **Erasing my data**.
4. Under **Anything to add**, write what you want, such as which detail is wrong (up to 1,000 characters).
5. Tap **Send request**. It says **Sent. Your organisation answers by ….**
6. **Your requests** lists each with its kind and state: **Open** with **Answer due by …**, or **Done** or **Refused** with **Their answer: …**.

Your organisation's admins answer the request on the portal. For a copy, they prepare a file of every record that refers to you; erasure removes your personal details and keeps the business records, and only once you have left.

### Download your copy

1. When your organisation marks your request for a copy done, you get **Your data request is done**: **Your copy is on My data, kept 7 days from when it was prepared. Their answer: …**.
2. Open **More** › **My data**. Above the form, **Download my copy** shows the file's size and **kept until …**.
3. Tap **Download my copy**. It opens in your browser, which saves the file (a ZIP).
4. Keep the file safe: it holds your personal data. The link lasts 24 hours; tap **Download my copy** again for a new one while the copy is kept.

After the 7 days, the copy is removed and **My data** says **Your copy was removed after 7 days. Ask again for a new one.**

## What the system checks

- You may have one open request of each kind at a time.
- The answer is due 30 days after the request; admins are reminded before it is due.
- An erasure can be completed only once your user has been deactivated.
- Your copy is offered only to you, only once your organisation has marked the request done, and only while it is kept (7 days from when it was prepared). Each download is in the audit log.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Connect to the internet to see or send requests.** | No signal | Move to signal and **Try again** |
| **You already have an open request of this kind. Wait for its answer before asking again.** | You already have an open request of that kind | Wait for its answer, or choose another kind |
| **The server could not be reached. Check your connection, then try again.** | No connection when sending or downloading | Try again with signal |
| **Your copy was removed after 7 days. Ask again for a new one.** | The copy's 7 days are over | Ask for **A copy of my data** again |
| Your request for a copy is **Open** but there is no **Download my copy** | Your organisation hasn't marked it done yet | Wait for **Your data request is done** |
