# Manager procedures

For managers and business heads: the people who look after a team in the field. You work on the web portal (**Daily work** and **Team** in the left rail) and on the phone's **Team** tab. A Manager sees their team: themselves and everyone below them in the organisation chart. A Business head and an Admin see the whole organisation. The procedures name each permission a step needs, and the default role that holds it; your admin may have changed the roles.

| Procedure | What it is for | Who |
|---|---|---|
| [Review submissions](review-submissions.md) | Approve or reject the forms your team fills, one at a time or the clean ones together; set automatic approval | Manager, Business head, Admin |
| [Decide approvals](approvals.md) | Decide leave, fixes, expense claims, orders, new accounts, location changes and journey plans in the inbox; hand over while you're away | Manager, Business head, Admin |
| [Follow the team on the live map](live-map-and-trails.md) | See who is on duty and where, spot stale positions, replay a person's day | Manager, Business head, Admin |
| [Read the attendance register](attendance.md) | Read the month's register and leave, export it, and decide fixes and leave | Manager, Business head, Admin |
| [Plan a person's month](journey-plans.md) | Build routes, plan visits, generate the month and approve it | Manager, Business head, Admin |
| [Set targets and account plans](targets-and-account-plans.md) | Set, copy and lock each person's targets; keep an account's monthly plan and forecast | Manager, Business head, Admin |
| [Follow the team's performance](team-performance.md) | Read Home, the team's month, a person's month and the leaderboard | Manager, Business head, Admin, Viewer |
