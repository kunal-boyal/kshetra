# Stage U2: the frame — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** the portal's rail, app bar and sign-in page, and the console's rail, look like the prototype's (`WebRail`, the app bar of every `Web*` artboard, `WebSignIn`, `ConsoleRail`).

**Architecture:**
- The rail's counts come from a light read beside the dashboard: `GET /v1/dashboard/waiting`. It is the dashboard's own `waiting` part, so it shares its schema and services. This is in place of the phase plan's `/v1/me/counts`: the counts are the dashboard's, and IAM may not reach review or approvals.
- The person's choices move behind their avatar, in a `MenuButton` (a disclosure button and panel) added to `@thelamp/ui-web`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-ui-revamp.md` § Stage U2; the U0 gallery's sign-in and every page's frame; the audit notes on the rail, the app bar and sign-in.

## Global constraints

- Every word goes in `en.ts` and `hi.ts`.
- The language toggle names the other language in itself ("हिंदी", "English") and carries `lang`.
- Nothing is lost:
  - Security, theme and sign-out move into the avatar menu;
  - the bell stays;
  - SSO stays on sign-in, behind the method switch;
  - privacy and deletion links stay.
- D103: the prototype's "you belong to two companies" notice is not built.
- Playwright tests that used the bar's Theme select open the avatar menu first.

## Review focus

1. **No permission, no badge.** A field user's rail has no Review or Approvals count, because the counts are null. Tested in the shell test.
2. **The menu.** It closes on Escape and on a click outside, and focus returns to the avatar. Tested in MenuButton.
3. **Hindi.** The toggle and the menu in Hindi at 390 px don't overflow the bar; the bar wraps. Tested in the shell test at 390.
4. **SSO required.** A tenant that requires SSO still lands on the SSO method after `SSO_REQUIRED`. Covered by the existing test, kept green.
5. **No logo.** The organisation chip shows initials from the display name, and nothing breaks with an empty name. Tested.
