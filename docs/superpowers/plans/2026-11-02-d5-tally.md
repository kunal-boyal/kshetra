- in: customers, stock items with their prices, sales invoices and receipts;

## What Tally offers

From Tally's documentation of its XML format (Tally calls it data interchange). To be confirmed against a real export and recorded in `docs/integrations/tally.md`.

### Exports (TallyPrime: Export › Masters, and Export › Transactions from the Day Book)

- **The file:** an `ENVELOPE` whose `REQUESTDESC` names the company (`SVCURRENTCOMPANY`), then `TALLYMESSAGE` elements, one master or voucher each.
- **Encoding:** UTF-8, or UTF-16 with a byte-order mark when Unicode is chosen. Tally writes control characters as character references (`&#4;`), which XML 1.0 forbids, so they are removed before parsing.
- **Masters:**
  - `GROUP`: its name and `PARENT`, so the sub-groups of Sundry Debtors are found;
  - `LEDGER`: `NAME` and its aliases (`LANGUAGENAME.LIST`), `GUID`, `ALTERID`, `PARENT`, the address, state, PIN code, GSTIN, phone and email;
  - `STOCKITEM`: name and aliases, `GUID`, `PARENT` (the stock group), `BASEUNITS`, HSN and the GST rate (`GSTDETAILS.LIST`), and prices by price level (`FULLPRICELIST.LIST`, each from a date);
  - `VOUCHERTYPE`: name and `PARENT`, so a renamed type such as "GST Sales" is still a sale.
- **Vouchers:** `VOUCHER` with `VCHTYPE`, `GUID`, `DATE` (YYYYMMDD), `VOUCHERNUMBER`, `PARTYLEDGERNAME`, `ISCANCELLED`, ledger entries with signed `AMOUNT`, and bill allocations (`BILLALLOCATIONS.LIST`: `NAME`, `BILLTYPE` such as New Ref or Agst Ref, `BILLCREDITPERIOD`).
- **Numbers as Tally writes them:** amounts are signed (a debit negative); quantities carry their unit (`" 12 Nos"`); rates carry theirs (`"450.00/Nos"`).

### Imports (Import › Transactions)

The same envelope with `TALLYREQUEST` Import Data and `REPORTNAME` Vouchers. A `Sales Order` voucher carries:

- `DATE`, `VOUCHERTYPENAME`, `VOUCHERNUMBER`, `REFERENCE` (our order number) and `PARTYLEDGERNAME`;
- one `ALLINVENTORYENTRIES.LIST` a line: `STOCKITEMNAME`, `RATE`, `ACTUALQTY`, `BILLEDQTY` and `AMOUNT`;
- the party's ledger entry for the total.

A sales order posts no sale and moves no stock in Tally until it becomes an invoice there, which keeps spec 2.2's rule.

**To confirm on a real export:**

- whether the GST rate sits on the item or its stock group in the customer's release;
- whether Tally matches a voucher re-imported with the same `REMOTEID` instead of making a second one;
- that `VOUCHERNUMBER` is kept when the Sales Order type numbers automatically.

## Global constraints

### Nothing reaches the customer's computer

- The cell never calls Tally. Files come up through the portal and go down through the `browser` seam, so D101 is untouched.
- Records still enter only as rows of import targets, as the connection (`Access.connector`), audited as `connector`.

### Files

- **The upload:** `POST /v1/erp-connections/{id}/uploads` takes the XML as the body (`application/xml`, `text/xml` or `application/octet-stream`), up to the 20 MB file limit, with an `Idempotency-Key`. Admins export a month of the Day Book at a time if a year is larger.
- **Kept:**
  - the file is stored through `MediaService` as an `import_file`, the kind it is;
  - a run of kind `upload` reads it on the `connectors` queue (`perTenant`);
  - uploaded files expire with `importFileRetentionDays` (`RecordRetention`).
- **Refused, as `ERP_REFUSED` with words the admin can act on:**
  - a file that isn't a Tally export;
  - a file from another company than the connection's (the first upload names it);
  - any `DOCTYPE` or entity declaration.

  Parsing never resolves entities.
- **Orders file:** `POST /v1/erp-connections/{id}/orders-file` makes a run of kind `push`. Its file is kept 7 days, like an export (`EXPORT_KEEP_DAYS`), and downloaded by the run's ID.

### Rules (BR-61)

Pure functions in `packages/shared/src/erp-tally.ts`, over plain objects that `TallyFile` (`apps/api/src/modules/erp/tally-file.ts`) reads from the XML:

- `tallyNumber()`, `tallyQuantity()`, `tallyRate()` and `tallyDate()`: Tally's signed amounts, quantities with units, rates per unit and dates, exact as decimal strings (FX-82).
- `debtorGroups()`: Sundry Debtors and every group under it, through the groups' parents (FX-83).
- `tallyCustomerRow()` (FX-84):
  - only ledgers in the debtor groups the mapping names;
  - the account code is the ledger's first alias that is a valid account code (see decision 1), else `no_customer_number`;
  - the territory comes from the ledger's group through `groupTerritories` (for example "Debtors - Pune" → `PUNE`), else the account's present territory, else the default.
- `tallyItemRow()` (FX-85):
  - the SKU is the stock item's first valid alias, else `no_sku`;
  - the unit is `BASEUNITS` through the unit mappings, the product is its stock group, HSN and the GST rate come from its GST details, else the default rate, else `no_tax_rate`;
  - prices come from the mapped price levels, the latest dated on or before today.
- `tallyInvoiceRow()` and `tallyReceiptRow()` (FX-86, FX-87):
  - vouchers whose type is, or descends from, Sales or Receipt (or the types the mapping names);
  - the invoice's amount is the party's entry, and its due date its credit period;
  - a receipt settles the invoices its Agst Ref allocations name, and the rest stays on account;
  - cancelled vouchers are cancelled.
- `tallyOrderFor()` and `tallyImportFile()` (FX-88):
  - the import file of accepted orders, lines from `acceptedLinesOut()`;
  - party and stock item names as Tally knows them, `REFERENCE` and `VOUCHERNUMBER` our order number, and `REMOTEID` our order's ID;
  - every value escaped.

### Changes to what Zoho and SAP built

- `ERP_PROVIDERS` gains `tally`. `POST /v1/erp-connections/tally` connects with nothing but the admin's word, and the company is fixed by the first upload.
- `erp_links` gains `external_name`, since Tally refers to ledgers and stock items by name: a rename in Tally keeps the link (by `GUID`) and updates the name.
- `ErpRuns` is 1,200 lines, so Tally's steps go in `tally-runs.ts` (`TallyReads`). They reach `settle()`, `links()`, the counts and problems through a small `RunKit` the run hands them, so nothing moves.
- The order consumer schedules no push for Tally: orders leave only when an admin makes the file.
- `ErpSettings` gains Tally's mappings:
  - `debtorGroups` (default Sundry Debtors);
  - `groupTerritories`;
  - `salesTypes` and `receiptTypes`;
  - `priceLevels` (each into one of the tenant's price lists);
  - `orderType` (default Sales Order).

### Duplicates out

- An order goes into one file only, marked in `erp_links` with the run that wrote it. "Include again" puts an order back for the next file.
- When a later Day Book upload holds a sales order whose `REFERENCE` is our number, the link is confirmed as in the books.

## Decisions for Kunal

1. **Account codes from Tally.** Tally ledgers have names with spaces, which an account code can't hold. *Recommended:* the ledger's alias, which is where Tally users usually keep a customer code. Ledgers without one are listed for the admin to fix in Tally. The other way, a code made from the name, breaks when a ledger is renamed and can collide.
2. **An XML parser.** *Recommended:* `@xmldom/xmldom`, already in the lockfile through SAML sign-in, added as the API's own dependency at its current release. It never fetches external entities, and DOCTYPEs are refused before it runs. This is a new direct dependency, so it needs your approval.
3. **Orders into Tally as sales orders.** *Recommended:* sales orders, not sales invoices, so nothing in Tally is posted until the customer's accountant converts them there.

## Review focus

1. **Odd files.** UTF-16, `&#4;`, a missing BOM, an export from another company, a Day Book with deleted or cancelled vouchers, a file of masters only: each is read or refused in words, never half-applied.
2. **Hostile files.** Entity expansion, a DOCTYPE, a 20 MB single line, deep nesting: refused before parsing or within bounded time and memory.
3. **Renames.** A ledger or stock item renamed in Tally keeps its account or item, and the next orders file uses the new name.
4. **Money.** Signs, paise and rates per unit are exact, and a receipt split over three invoices settles each.
5. **Orders out.** Making a file twice never puts an order in both. An order whose customer or item came from no upload waits as `not_in_books`.
