# Stage M4: orders — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Taking an order on the phone reads as the prototype's `AppOrder` and `AppOrderReview`: a compact bar naming the account, a search field with its icon, categories as chips, the items as rows with a hued chip and a −/+ stepper, and the cart held at the foot as a bar ("4 items · ₹19,780 + GST · Review"); then a separate Review step with each line's sum, the totals with a ruled Total, whether the order is within credit, the note, and "Place the order · ₹total" held at its foot.

**Architecture:** No API change. `OrderScreen` keeps its cart and gains a `step` (`items` or `review`); the review's way back, and Android's back button while reviewing, return to the items with the cart kept. `@thelamp/ui-mobile` gains `QuantityStepper`, a leading `icon` and `labelHidden` on `TextField`, and a `success` tone on `Notice`; each goes into the design system in both places.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-ui-revamp.md` § Stage M4; the prototype's `AppOrder.dc.html` and `AppOrderReview.dc.html`; decision D78 for pricing and order states.

## Global constraints

- Pricing stays the server's: every line through `priceCart()` (`priceLine()` by the catalogue's `rounding`), totals through `orderTotals()` and `gstHalves()`; quantities are decimal strings, stepped with `scaled()` and `unscaled()` at 3 places, never `Number`.
- An item is on one line (BR-13). The stepper steps by 1 in the line's unit (the base unit for a new line); stepping below 1 removes the line. Pressing the quantity opens the item's editor (unit and exact quantity), as the row did before.
- "Deliver by" stays out (D104: delivery dates need their own decision); the prototype's order states don't apply (D78).
- The cart bar shows the taxable sum with "+ GST"; Review shows Subtotal, CGST, SGST and the Total.
- Credit: within the limit says how much was free before this order (`success`); over it keeps today's `orders.over_credit` (`pending`); no balance or no limit says nothing.
- Placing works as today: a fix is needed; offline queues unless `ordersAllowedOffline` is off, which posts at once.

## Review focus

5. **Hindi at 390 px.** The cart bar, the stepper rows and the review totals fit without clipping. Checked in the browser.
