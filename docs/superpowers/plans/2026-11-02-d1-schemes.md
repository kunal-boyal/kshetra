# Phase 4 step D1: schemes and promotions on orders — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** the company's trade schemes are applied to orders automatically, the same way on the phone (offline), at the order desk and on the server. The four kinds are a percentage off, a flat amount per unit, quantity slabs and free goods. Each scheme is scoped by item or category, account type, territory and dates. Each line gets the one scheme best for the customer. The server reprices every order and refuses with `PRICE_CHANGED` what the device showed otherwise.

**Kunal's answers (2026-11-01), decision D127.** He confirmed my recommended design in place of real schemes, which a customer's will check later:

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Kinds | Percentage; flat per unit; quantity slabs (for example 2%, 4% and 6% at 100, 250 and 500); free goods (buy X get Y, rounded down) |
| Scope | Item or category, account type, territory, and a date range |
| Stacking | One scheme per line; the best for the customer applies on its own |
| Tax | A discount lowers the taxable value; free goods are a line at zero price |
| Server | Reprices every order; a scheme the device showed differently is `PRICE_CHANGED` |
| Metrics | Order value counts after the discount |
| Not in v1 | Cash discount, scheme budgets and caps |

**Spec:** M10 (orders), BR-10 to BR-15, BR-19; D77, D78, D96, D109. New: BR-55.

## Global constraints

- **Rules:** pure functions in `packages/shared/src/schemes.ts` (BR-55), on exact decimals (`scaled`, `unscaled`), never `Number`:
  - `schemeApplies(scheme, line, account, date)`: the item or its category is named, or neither is; the account's type is named, or none is; the account's territory is the named territory or under it, or none is named; the date is within `validFrom`..`validTo` (both on the tenant's clock; `validTo` may be open).
  - `schemeBenefit(scheme, line, orderLines)`:
    - **percentage:** off the line's taxable after the price list's discount. `round(taxable × pct / 100, 2)`.
    - **flat per unit:** `round(amount × base units, 2)`, never more than the taxable.
    - **slabs:** the highest slab reached, by the base units of every line in the order the scheme covers. The percentage is taken as above.
    - **free goods:** `floor(base units covered ÷ buy) × get` base units of the free item, the same item when none is named, at price 0.
  - `bestScheme(schemes, line, …)`: the largest benefit. Free goods are valued at the free item's price for that account. Ties go to the earliest `validFrom`, then the key.
  - `priceOrder(lines, schemes, context)`: every line priced by BR-10 to BR-12 with its scheme's discount taken off the taxable before tax, plus a free line for each free-goods benefit. It returns the totals of BR-13.
  - Fixtures FX-55 to FX-58:
    - a percentage scheme against a flat one on the same line;
    - slabs at 100, 250 and 500 across two items of one category;
    - free goods rounded down;
    - a scheme out of its dates or territory.
- **Lines:**
  - `order_lines` gains `scheme_key`, `scheme_name` (both languages), `scheme_discount` (numeric(18,2), 0 when none) and `free_of_line_id` (the line a free line came from).
  - A free line is its own line at price 0, tax 0, total 0, with `free_of_line_id` set. BR-13's one-line-per-item rule leaves free lines out.
  - The snapshot (BR-15) keeps the scheme as it was.
- **Quotes:** each line's `quoted` gains `scheme: { key, discount } | null`, and free lines are quoted as `free: [{ itemId, baseUnits, ofItemId }]`. BR-14's `quoteDifferences()` adds `scheme`, so a scheme that changed, started or ended since the device priced the order is `PRICE_CHANGED`.
- **Tables** (RLS, keys led by `tenant_id`, cross-tenant fixtures, `rowVersionColumn()` for sync): `cell.schemes` with these columns:
  - `tenant_id`, `id`, `key`, `name` (jsonb, en and hi) and `kind`;
  - `rule` (jsonb: percent, amount, slabs, buy, get and free item), and `item_ids[]`, `category_ids[]`, `account_type_keys[]`, `territory_ids[]`;
  - `valid_from`, `valid_to` (null for open), `active`, `version`, the change columns and `row_version`.

  A scheme's key never changes. A change is audited and raises its version; orders already placed keep their snapshot.
- **Permission:** `scheme.manage`, in the orders module (`MODULE_PERMISSIONS`). It goes to admins and business heads in the templates that order (agri, FMCG, building, pharma), and to existing admins by migration. Its audit actions are `scheme.created`, `scheme.updated` and `scheme.ended`.
- **Routes:** `GET /v1/schemes` and `PUT /v1/schemes/{id}` (`scheme.manage`, idempotent by ID). Schemes are switched off, never removed.
- **Phone:** the `schemes` sync entity carries the active schemes whose dates have not passed, each person getting the ones that could reach accounts they see. Its scope hashes the scheme versions, as prices do. The cart (`/order/<accountId>`):
  - prices with `priceOrder()`;
  - shows the scheme on each line and the free lines under it;
  - sends what it showed.

  A refused order is repriced with `repriceRefused()` as today.
- **Portal:**
  - Settings › Schemes: a list, and an editor for kind, rule, scope and dates, with an example worked out live.
  - The order page and the orders register show each line's scheme and the free lines.
  - The order desk prices with `priceOrder()`.
- **Reports:**
  - The orders register gains the scheme and scheme discount columns.
  - A new `scheme_summary` report shows, per scheme for the month, the orders, the lines, the discount given and the free goods by item.
- **Metrics:** order value is the taxable after the scheme discount, as today's `taxable` becomes. Nothing else changes.
- **Demo:** each tenant that orders gets three schemes from its own catalogue (a percentage on one category, slabs on a fast item, buy-10-get-1 on another), so the orders it places carry them.
- **Words:** English and Hindi; scheme kinds as `scheme.kind_*`.

## Choices of mine, confirmed by Kunal on 2026-11-02

1. **Slabs count across the order:** the slab is chosen by the base units of all the scheme's lines in the order, not each line alone. "250 packs of any neem product" is how slabs are usually written.
2. **On top of the price list:** a scheme's percentage comes off the price-list net rate's taxable; it never replaces the list's own discount.
3. **Who manages:** `scheme.manage` for admins and business heads.
4. **Three pull requests** instead of two. This step touches pricing everywhere:
   - the rules and the server;
   - the portal;
   - the phone and the demo.

## Review focus

1. **A scheme ends while an order waits offline.** It is refused with `PRICE_CHANGED`, repriced on the phone, and sent again. Tested in `device.int.test.ts`.
2. **Two schemes tie.** The same one is chosen on the phone and the server, by the tie rule. Tested.
3. **Free goods of another item the account has no price for.** The free line is still made at 0, and its value for "best" is 0. Tested.
4. **Rounding.** A percentage off a per-unit-first or end-rounded line matches to the paisa between the phone and the server, through the shared function. Tested against FX-55.
5. **The orders module off.** Schemes, their routes, sync and report go with it. Tested.
