# Phase 4 step A1: search across the product — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** a search box that finds what a person may see, in one place (D96, D109):
- people, accounts, orders and forms;
- the screens they may open;
- in the portal's bar (`/` to focus it), and on the phone.

**Architecture:**
- **One API route, `GET /v1/search?q=`, in a new `search` module.** It reaches each kind only through the owning module's exported service, which already narrows by scope:
  - `UsersService.list()` with `q`;
  - `AccountsService.list()` with `q`;
  - `OrdersService.list()` with a new `number` filter;
  - `FormsService.list()`, filtered by name.

  A kind the caller may not list is `null`, not empty. That covers a missing permission (`user.view`, `account.view`, `form.manage`) and a module switched off (orders).
- **Screens are found in the app, not on the server:** the portal's navigation entries the caller may open, by their names in both languages.
- **The phone searches what it keeps** (people, accounts and its own orders in SQLite), so search works offline. It needs no new route.

**Spec:** D96 (the outside demo's review), D109; spec 8.2 (the portal's bar), M1 (scopes).

## Global constraints

- **Query:** `q` is 2 to 100 characters, trimmed. Each kind returns at most 5 matches, so the box stays short; each section links to its full list filtered by `q` where one exists (people, accounts).
- **Privacy:** people match a mobile number, as the People list does, but the answer never carries a number, email or anything beyond code, name, designation and status. A search is never logged with its words, as query strings never are.
- **Orders:** matched by number only (`1042`, `#1042`), within what the caller may see (`OrdersService.visible()`). An account's orders are a click away from the account.
- **Clients and support:** API clients are refused, because the route is for people (no `@ForApiClients`). Vendor support under a grant searches as its grant allows, through the same services.
- **Words:** the box, the sections and empty states in English and Hindi; screen names are the rail's own strings.

## Review focus

1. **A person outside the caller's scope** is never found, even by exact name or number. Tested.
2. **No permission for a kind:** that section is absent (`null`), not empty. Tested for a field person.
3. **Orders switched off:** no orders section, and no error. Tested.
4. **Another tenant's records** are never found. The cross-tenant fixture covers this.
5. **Hindi names and words:** a Devanagari query finds a form by its Hindi name, and the box fits at 390 px. Tested; checked in the browser.
