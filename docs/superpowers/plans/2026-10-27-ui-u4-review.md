# Stage U4: the review queue — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Review looks like the prototype's `WebReview`: a compact queue beside the chosen submission's detail, status chips with counts, "Approve N clean", and a detail card with flags, the geofence pill, values, a strip of photos with a mini map, and a note field always shown.

**Architecture:** One API task (counts, the "with flags" filter, the review rules and each account's geofence in the queue's answer), then the page, rebuilt on `SplitDetail`, `DataTable`, `FilterChips`, `DescriptionList` and the design system's `ks-flag`, `ks-pill`, `ks-photos` and `ks-actions`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-ui-revamp.md` § Stage U4; the prototype's `WebReview.dc.html` in `tools/ui-compare/prototype/`.

## Global constraints

- Decisions stay `POST /v1/submissions/{id}/review` and `POST /v1/reviews/bulk`; a rejection and a change of decision still need a note of `REVIEW_NOTE_MIN` characters (BR in `reviewRefusal()`).
- Bulk approval still approves only pending submissions without flags, at most `BULK_REVIEW_MAX` at once, and only for `submission.review_bulk`.
- Nothing is lost: every filter, the review history, auto-approval's time, the integrity warning, duplicate photos, the full map and the rules section.
- "Send back" is out of scope (feature decision 4).
- Every word in en and hi.

## Review focus

1. **An empty queue.** No detail card, the empty state, no "Approve 0 clean". Tested.
2. **Deciding the chosen row.** After approving, the next waiting submission is chosen, and the counts move. Tested.
3. **A submission without an account or a verified location.** No mini map; the pill says the geofence is unknown or is left out. Tested.
4. **Later pages.** Counts come with the first page only (`summary` is null after), and the chips keep the first page's counts. Tested in the API.
5. **390 px.** The detail stacks below the queue, the photos wrap, the table scrolls in its box. Checked in the browser.

### Task 1: the queue's answer (API)

**Files:**
- `packages/shared/src/schemas/review.ts`:
  - `ReviewQueueQuery` gains `flagged: 'yes' | 'no'` (optional): pending ones with any flag, or none;
  - `ReviewQueueResult` gains `summary: { counts: { pending, flagged, clean, approved, rejected }, autoApproval: boolean, delayHours: int } | null`, given with the first page only, counted under the same filters but status, flag and flagged;
  - `ReviewItem` gains `geofenceAt: { location: GeoPoint, radiusM: int } | null`: the account's verified location and its radius.
- `apps/api/src/modules/field/submissions.service.ts`: the queue's conditions shared by `reviewRows()` and a new `reviewCounts()`.
- `apps/api/src/modules/accounts/accounts.service.ts`: `geofences(tx, tenantId, ids): Map<id, { location, radiusM }>`, verified locations only, the radius by `geofenceRadius()`.
- `apps/api/src/modules/review/review.service.ts`: puts them together; the rules through `TenantConfigService`.
- [ ] Test (`test/review.int.test.ts`): counts with a flagged and a clean pending submission; `flagged=yes` lists only the flagged; `summary` is null on a cursor page; `geofenceAt` carries the account's radius.
- [ ] `pnpm openapi`.

### Task 2: the page

**Files:** `apps/web/src/review/review-page.tsx` (split into `review-page.tsx`, `review-detail.tsx`), `review.test.tsx`, `data.ts`, the strings.

- Header: title "Review"; meta "14 waiting · clean ones approve themselves after 48 hours" (or just the count with auto-approval off); toolbar chips Waiting · n, With flags · n, Approved · n, Rejected · n; a ghost "More filters" chip that shows the form, flag, geofence and date fields; "Approve 9 clean" (tonal) for bulk reviewers, which asks first and approves the clean ones, up to `BULK_REVIEW_MAX`.
- Queue: `DataTable` with Person (Avatar and a button that chooses the row), Form, Account, When (time today, else the date), Flags (the first as `ks-flag`, `+n` for more); the chosen row selected; Load more.
- Detail (`ks-card raised`): form name, "person · account · when", status badge; flags as `ks-flag` (mock location, integrity and repeated photos `danger`); the geofence as `ks-pill inside|outside`; auto-approval's time; values in `DescriptionList`; photos 112×84 with time and distance tags, then a mini map (an SVG of the ring and the check-in dot) when `geofenceAt` is known, and "Show on map" for the full map; the history; "Note to Anil" always shown; `ks-actions` Reject and Approve, or the other decision for a decided one.
- [ ] Tests: chips carry counts and choose the status; "With flags" asks `flagged=yes`; choosing a row shows its detail; approving chooses the next; a rejection without a note shows the field error; "Approve 9 clean" confirms and posts the clean IDs; the mini map is drawn only with `geofenceAt`.
