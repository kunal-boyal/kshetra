# UI revamp: the built product, made to match the prototype (phase plan)

> **For agentic workers:** this is a phase plan, not a task plan. Each stage below gets its own task plan (superpowers:writing-plans) when Kunal starts it, executed with superpowers:subagent-driven-development or superpowers:executing-plans. Nothing here is built until Kunal approves the phase and then each stage.

**Goal:** every screen of the web portal, the console and the field app looks and reads like the approved prototype (https://claude.ai/artifact/5gU3wR97hjoXsho2q7qZGj), down to small details, without losing anything the product does that the prototype doesn't.

**Architecture:** the design system already has every class the prototype uses (all 67 `ks-*` classes are in `packages/design-tokens/components/bundle.css`). What's missing is the layer between the classes and the screens: reusable composites (avatars, rank chips, filter chips, stat tiles, split views, timelines) that the prototype composes and the screens never got, plus a few list counts and fields the API doesn't return yet. So the phase builds that layer first, then redoes screens a few at a time, each against a side-by-side screenshot of the prototype.

**Tech stack:** React + Vite, Tailwind for layout only, `@thelamp/ui-web` on the design system's `bundle.css`; Expo React Native with `@thelamp/ui-mobile`; NestJS API with zod schemas in `packages/shared`.

**Spec:** the prototype artifact above (Main.dc.html is its index), the design system artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/REeP6ZXP72DN9kfhVHbPT3) and `packages/design-tokens/README.md`. The prototype is the reference for look and layout; `docs/spec.md` and `docs/decisions.md` stay the reference for behaviour.

## Why the product looks basic today

The prototype and the product share the brand, the tokens and the classes; the gap is composition:

1. **Every screen is one pattern.** `PageHeader` → a grid of labelled `TextField`/`SelectField` filters → `DataTable`. The prototype puts the period in the title, a one-line `ks-meta` summary under it, filters as a row of chips with counts, stat tiles or a side panel beside the table, and a selected row with a detail card. The labelled filter grid alone is why most pages read like forms.
2. **People are names, not people.** No initials avatars, no hue per person, no rank chips, no "you" row. The prototype marks every person and place with an `ks-ico` chip.
3. **Status is text.** The prototype uses `ks-badge` tones, `ks-flag`, `ks-pill`, live dots and `ks-steps` timelines; the product often prints words.
4. **Pages are single columns.** The prototype uses two columns (list and detail, content and summary panel) on almost every page; the product stacks full-width cards.
5. **Some figures don't exist yet.** Leaderboard rows have no territory, previous rank or per-metric figures; lists return no per-status counts or totals; there's no team attendance register. These are named in each stage, with the endpoint and schema to extend.

The leaderboard shows all five. Prototype: "Performance · October" with a meta line, ranking-group chips with counts ("SMEs · 24"), a "How the score is made" weight bar, and a table of rank, person, territory, each metric's attainment, score and movement since last week, with the viewer's row highlighted. Portal: a month input over a three-column table (rank, person, score).

## Global constraints

- The brand stays Field (decision D72): Forest and harvest gold, Mukta, the pin-and-flame mark. Colours, spacing, radii, type and motion come from the tokens only; no hex values, no new fonts.
- Every new or changed component lands in the design system in both places in the same piece of work: `packages/design-tokens` and the design system artifact, read back byte for byte.
- Text in English and Hindi, through the terminology resolver; no industry words in code.
- WCAG 2.2 AA: axe clean in Playwright; 4.5:1 text contrast in Light, Dark and Outdoor; visible focus; `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured; touch targets at least 44 px on the phone.
- Layouts work at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390 px wide with no horizontal page scroll; tables scroll inside their own container.
- Native controls stay native (selects, checkboxes), as the Picker does (PR #139).
- Nothing the product does is lost: every "Keep" in the audit stays, moved if need be.
- Where the prototype contradicts a decision, the decision wins. Known cases:
  - the sign-in's "you belong to two companies" notice (D103);
  - the prototype's order states Placed/Confirmed/Dispatched/Invoiced (D78 keeps submitted/accepted/…);
  - the "Phase 2/3" pills in its rail.
- Each stage is one pull request on the usual workflow: Auto-fix, `pnpm verify` before merging, Kunal's word to merge.

## Review focus (what most likely breaks, tested in every stage)

1. **Hindi is longer.** Chips, tile labels, tab names and rank rows must wrap or truncate cleanly in Hindi. Each stage's Playwright test renders its screen in Hindi at 390 px and asserts no horizontal overflow.
2. **Outdoor theme.** Pastel hues and tinted cards wash out in glare. Each new component's preview card shows all three themes, and the token tests' contrast pairs gain each new pairing.
3. **Empty, loading and one-row states.**
   - A leaderboard with one person, a team with nobody on duty, a month with no plan.
   - Each redesigned screen keeps an `EmptyState` and a skeleton, and has a test for the empty case.
4. **Long names and big numbers.** A 40-character account name, ₹1,23,45,678 and 1,443 rows must not break a row, tile or chip. There is a test per component with a long value.
5. **Tenant accents.** Selected rows, rings and chips use the tenant's accent from `deriveAccent()`. Each stage checks one non-default brand colour.

## How each stage runs

1. Write the stage's task plan (files, component signatures, tests), from this plan's checklist for it.
2. Build components first (with their design-system card and a unit test), then the screens.
3. Screenshot the prototype page and the built page side by side, in Light and Dark at 1440 and 390, with Playwright (stage U0's tool). Publish them as one gallery artifact for Kunal.
4. `pnpm verify`, open the pull request, Kunal reviews the gallery and says merge, or lists what's still off; those become the stage's last commits.

Sizes: S is a day or less, M two or three days, L about a week.

## Decisions

Kunal approved the plan on 2026-10-27 with each recommendation below (decision D104).

1. **Order of work.** Recommended: portal first (U0 → U3 → U4 → …), with the phone's M1 and M2 starting once U1 is in. Alternative: phone first, because field people use it all day.
2. **The phone's tab set.**
   - Prototype: Home, Plan, Orders, Team, More.
   - Today: Home, Accounts, Forms, Team, Me.
   - Recommended: the prototype's tabs, with Accounts and Forms reached from Home's "Start something" and from More.
3. **Language switch in the portal's bar.** The prototype's one-tap "हिंदी" toggle, or the Picker just built (PR #139). Recommended: the toggle (two languages need no list), keeping the Picker for the theme.
4. **Features the prototype shows that the product doesn't have.** These are new capabilities, not UI, so each needs its own decision and is outside this phase unless chosen:
   - collecting payments on the phone;
   - one monthly claim with many bills;
   - "Send back" in review and in plans;
   - "Invited" and "Phone reset" person states;
   - order delivery dates and invoice matching;
   - a tenant-wide outstanding and collections page;
   - global search;
   - email codes at sign-in;
   - a form's draft and version history;
   - start and finish times on attendance fixes.
5. **Numbering.** This becomes a phase of its own ("phase U") beside phase 3's remaining owner items, recorded as decision D104.
